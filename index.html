FAQ

Clear answers.

Do we need to replace our existing tools?

Not necessarily. Existing advertising platforms, CRM, website, email and messaging tools can be connected where practical.

What can AI automate?

Depending on the workflow, AI can assist with lead prioritization, segmentation, personalization, recommendations, anomaly detection and repeatable marketing actions.

Is everything fully automated?

No. Automation should be applied where it is useful and controlled. Important business decisions can remain subject to human review.

How long does implementation take?

It depends on the scope, integrations, data sources and existing marketing setup. A clear implementation plan is created after discovery.

Who owns our accounts and data?

The intended model is for client-owned accounts, data, advertising assets, dashboards and workspaces to remain under the client's control.
